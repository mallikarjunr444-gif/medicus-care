## 🛡️ Medical Safety & Regulatory Compliance Notice

Medicus Care is an artificial intelligence pre-screening and healthcare educational platform designed to empower patient-doctor discussions. **It is not an autonomous licensed doctor.** It does not provide definitive medical diagnoses or autonomous pharmaceutical prescriptions. In acute medical emergencies, immediately dial **112** (India) or visit the nearest emergency hospital.
